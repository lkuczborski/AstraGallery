# Astra — Rooms of Light, second arrangement

Original continuous instrumental music for the 120-second Astra Gallery walkthrough. Warm piano, soft strings, harp and a restrained rhythmic pulse follow the visitor from the street through the rooms. Forty-six bars; no repeated audio loop, vocals or artist imitation.
